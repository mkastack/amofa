# AMOFA storefront

## Scope and visitor mode
Homepage plus the connected shop, product detail, bag, checkout, About, Contact, FAQ, Shipping & Returns, Privacy, and Terms routes, restyled as one system. Persuade: help students and families in Ghana, teachers, and other stationery buyers understand the two products and shop them.

## Visitor, job, evidence, and constraints
Visitors evaluate the AMOFA Abacus Pencil and AMOFA Ink-Cleaning Eraser, then start a purchase. Evidence is limited to product names and stated brand priorities. Prices, specifications, inventory, delivery and return terms, photography, contact details, testimonials, and payment-provider availability are unconfirmed. GHS pricing stays explicitly pending; MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AT Money appear only as planned options. No backend: never claim a payment, order, signup, or message succeeded. User decisions (2026-11-16): Apple Store-led structure blended with plug.tech color energy; the official AMOFA palette in tokens.css; logo-free drawn AMOFA concept renders replace stock photos that showed other brands.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An Apple Store-style shop where AMOFA's two products sit as lit objects on quiet shelves, and Plug-style saturated blue fields mark the moments that sell. It refuses Plug's campaign-carousel homepage and the split headline-plus-product hero.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white #F7F6F2 ground; 400x500 shelf cards measured from apple.com/store (28px radius, shadow 2px 4px 12px rgb(0 0 0 / 8%), 28px padding) tinted from the official palette (tone-blue #E6ECF8, tone-stone #E9E7E1, white) with drawn product renders bleeding off the bottom edge; Mona Sans 600 headings whose lead phrase is AMOFA navy #1C3A7C and whose continuation is gray #767A80; AMOFA blue #315CBB pill actions; full-bleed blue fields for the announcement gradient, the demo stage, promo tiles, and the newsletter; Apple-style numbered footnotes in the footer carry every pending fact.

STORY: Visitors see "Store." and both products at once, add one to the bag from the first shelf, then slide the abacus beads and erase a line of ink on the blue stage, and know exactly what is still pending without doubting what is real.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue gradient announcement strip; 44px Apple-style nav with measured hover flyouts. A 2:1 hero row: the blue "Back to school, sorted." campaign banner (Shop now pill, See deals link, still life of five renders) beside two promo tiles (new Abacus Pencil with price, deals up to 18% off). The benefits strip and the top of the shop-by-category cards follow inside 1440x900. (Owner request 2026-11-17: classic online-shop homepage, demo checkout that completes in test mode.)

FORM: User-pinned Apple Store + plug.tech blend, Apple-led; no direction roll was run. Signature interaction: abacus bead slider (pointer + keyboard slider) and an ink-erasing canvas, with spring motion on the beads only. seed: none (pinned). Pinned by the request "BLENDED PROTION OF APPLE STORE ON APPLE.COM AND PLUG.TECH" and the confirmed answer "Apple-led (Apple Store structure: Store header, product icon row, side-scrolling card shelves; Plug adds colour energy)".
