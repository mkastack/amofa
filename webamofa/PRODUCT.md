# Product

## Platform
web

## Stack
Existing Next.js App Router application with React and TypeScript.

## Users
Students and families in Ghana, alongside teachers and other stationery buyers.

## Product Purpose
AMOFA sells innovative stationery and educational products, beginning with an abacus pencil and an ink-cleaning eraser.

## Positioning
The products are intended to bring new functions to familiar stationery. The exact mechanisms and product claims remain to be confirmed.

## Operating Context
Customers shop from Ghana. Store pricing and checkout should use GHS and account for Ghanaian Mobile Money options.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Initial products: AMOFA Abacus Pencil and AMOFA Ink-Cleaning Eraser.
- The owner believes the abacus pencil may be the first of its kind in Africa; this has not been independently verified and must not be presented as an objective claim until substantiated.
- Prices, specifications, inventory, delivery terms, return terms, and payment-provider availability are not yet confirmed.
- Mobile Money options under consideration: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AT Money. No payment provider is currently confirmed.
- Checkout runs as a test-mode demo (owner decision, 2026-11-17): the full flow (delivery details, delivery option, Mobile Money provider and number, approve-on-phone, order confirmation) completes, but every checkout screen says plainly that payments are simulated, no money is taken, and no order reaches AMOFA. A real provider (Paystack or Hubtel) replaces the simulated step before launch.
- All prices, sale prices, delivery fees, and the GH₵150 free-delivery threshold are sample values (footnotes 1 and 4), including sample prices for the Abacus Pencil (GH₵12.00) and Ink-Cleaning Eraser (GH₵7.50).
- Sample catalogue (added 2026-11-16 at the owner's request): ten placeholder stationery listings (pencils, erasers, sharpener, notebooks, ruler, geometry set, crayons, pencil case) with sample GHS prices and sample stock levels, so the store can be previewed fully stocked. They are flagged by footnote 5 and must be replaced with the real catalogue, prices, and inventory before launch. The Abacus Pencil and Ink-Cleaning Eraser keep "to be confirmed" price and availability.

## Brand Commitments
The brand is AMOFA, a modern, innovative, trustworthy stationery and education products company. Priorities are quality, simplicity, innovation, and reliability.

## Evidence on Hand
No approved product photography, prices, specifications, real testimonials, certifications, company history, or verified shipping and returns details have been supplied. Any sample testimonials must be clearly identified as samples.

## Product Principles
- Make purchasing straightforward for Ghanaian customers.
- Keep product facts and claims verifiable.
- Treat stationery as thoughtfully designed products, not generic commodities.
- Be explicit about any feature or transaction that is not yet available.
