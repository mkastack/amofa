Visitors need to evaluate the AMOFA Abacus Pencil and AMOFA Ink-Cleaning Eraser, then start a purchase. Available evidence is limited to the product names and the user's stated brand priorities. OWN-WORLD: Use the reference's spacious product staging, simple navigation, and confident white-to-pale-mint surfaces, translated into an AMOFA identity with deep teal typography and a restrained warm accent. Product names and actions remain plain, never wrapped in a metaphor.
